The single reference for how the perocho brand looks, reads and behaves. Use it for the site, perocho-repairs, slides, résumés, social posts, documents, side projects and anything else that carries the name.

perocho.com runs on v2.0 and is the source of truth: when its tokens change, update this guide from src/styles/global.css.

01 Brand principles

Warm, not sterileOff-white paper and warm near-black instead of pure #fff/#000. Every neutral leans slightly warm.
One accentTomato is the only brand color. It marks what matters: the eighth leaf, the period, a link underline, the active state.
Quiet structureHairline borders and whitespace do the work. No shadows, gradients or heavy fills.
Engineer’s precisionMonospace for data and metadata, exact numbers, plain sentences. Every logo measurement comes from the letters.

02 Logo

“Perocho” has seven letters, and the family has seven members. Each letter, and each dark leaf in the symbol, stands for one of them. The period is the eighth character and the tomato leaf is the eighth leaf: the open seat, for the client, the reader, or whoever joins next. “Ocho” is Spanish for eight.

Construction

Sizes are fractions of the wordmark’s font size, so the lockup scales as one piece. The dashed box is the clear space.

Symbol

  • Eight leaves in a ring, 45° apart, one pointing straight up. Seven are text-colored; the second clockwise from the top is tomato. Always exactly one.
  • Height is 0.86 of the font size: from the bottom of the “p” to the top of the “h”. Its center sits 0.28 above the baseline.
  • The gap between two leaves is half the thickness of a letter stroke.
  • Minimum size 16 px. Alone, it is the favicon; in paper and tomato on an ink tile (corner radius ≈ 22% of the side) it is the avatar and app icon.

Wordmark & lockup

  • “perocho.” in lowercase Geist SemiBold (600), letter-spacing −0.04em. The period is part of the mark and is always tomato.
  • Space between the symbol’s right tip and the “p” is 0.33 of the font size.
  • Keep clear space of half the symbol’s height on every side.
  • Lockup minimum: 24 px font size (about 120 px wide). Below that, use the symbol alone.
  • Use the logo file, never retype the wordmark. In running text the name is “Perocho”; use “Alexander Perocho” in formal contexts (copyright lines, résumé header).
  • One-color print: all leaves and the period in one color.

Don’t

Don’t move the tomato leaf. It is always upper right.

Don’t color more than one leaf. There is one open seat.

Don’t twist the leaves. They point straight out.

Don’t add or remove leaves. Eight, always.

Don’t stretch or squash it. Scale it as one piece.

Don’t place it on tomato. The eighth leaf disappears.

Supporting values

  • Accent soft --accent-soft: tomato at 12% opacity (light, rgba(255,84,54,.12)) / 16% (dark, rgba(255,84,54,.16)). Background for badges and highlights only, with accent-text on top.

Rules

  • Weights in use: 400 body, 500 buttons/sub-roles/eyebrows, 600 headings and emphasis. Nothing heavier.
  • At most one accent word per view, in accent-text. Usually a name or a single key word. The v1 italic serif is retired.
  • Reading width (measure) is 680 px, about 70 characters.
  • Dates are written Mon D, YYYY (e.g. “Oct 19, 2026”) and set in mono.

Substitutes

Both families are free on Google Fonts. Where custom fonts are unavailable (email, some office tools):

Imagery

  • Images get the 10 px radius and a 1 px border; hero images sit in a surface-colored well, max 420 px tall, object-fit: contain.
  • Portraits are circular with a 1 px border, tilted −3°, rotating to +3° and 1.04× on hover.
  • Prefer real photos and real screenshots over stock or illustration. Warm, natural light suits the palette.
  • Icons: simple 16-unit glyphs at 18 px, currentColor, muted by default and accent on hover.

Motion

  • Default transition: 0.15 s ease on color, border, background and underline thickness.
  • Portrait: 0.3 s ease. Status dot pulse: 2.4 s ease-out, infinite.
  • Movement is small: 3 px arrow nudge, 3° tilt. Nothing slides in or bounces.
  • Respect prefers-reduced-motion: stop looping animations.
  • Focus: 2 px accent outline, 2 px offset, on every interactive element.

07 Voice & writing

  • First person, conversational, specific. “I build the infrastructure that gets machine learning models from training into production and keeps them healthy once they’re there.”
  • Lead with the answer. Post descriptions state the finding: “The answer was forever, for three separate reasons.”
  • Real numbers over adjectives. “6 EKS clusters across 2 AWS regions”, “$66K/yr”, not “many” or “significant”.
  • Name the tools. Kubernetes, Triton, Prometheus, Helm, Terraform, written with their official casing.
  • Sentence case for section headings, buttons and UI labels (“Recent writing”, “All posts →”). Title Case for blog post titles (“When Terraform Wants to Replace Every Node at Once”). Uppercase only for eyebrow labels.
  • Use colons and plain sentences for asides; avoid em-dash-heavy prose.
  • Arrows (→ ←) for directional links, middots (·) to separate metadata.
  • Tone: candid and a little self-deprecating about mistakes, never hype. No “passionate”, “rockstar”, “leveraging synergies”.

08 Applying the brand

Slides & presentations

  • Light bg #f6f2ea by default; dark #1a1614 for evening talks or demos.
  • Title: Geist 600, −0.03em, with at most one accent word. Small mono eyebrow above (section name or date).
  • Big numbers styled like the stats row. Code in Geist Mono on surface with a hairline border.
  • Symbol bottom-left on content slides; lockup on the title and closing slides.

Résumé & documents

  • Always light mode. Lockup top-left, name in Geist 600, contact line in mono, muted.
  • Section headings as uppercase eyebrows with a trailing hairline (like “01 Brand principles” above).
  • Tomato only in the logo, link underlines and timeline markers. Body text stays #1a1614.
  • Skills as chips or a mono comma list. Dates right-aligned in mono.

Social & thumbnails

  • OG / link images: 1200 × 630, bg or surface fill, post title in Geist 600, date in mono, lockup bottom-left.
  • Announcement cards may use an ink fill (#1a1614) with paper text. Tomato is never a full background.
  • Avatar: the portrait photo; fall back to the symbol tile.

Code, terminals & side projects

  • Syntax themes: GitHub Light / GitHub Dark (what the site uses via Shiki).
  • New sites: copy the tokens below verbatim, support prefers-color-scheme, no client JS unless it earns its place.
  • Set theme-color meta to #f6f2ea / #1a1614.
  • Sub-brands: set the descriptor in Geist Mono caps after the lockup, muted (“perocho. REPAIRS”). The symbol never changes.

Do

  • Use role tokens, and test both light and dark.
  • Keep tomato under ~3% of the area.
  • Separate with whitespace and 1 px hairlines.
  • Put dates, versions and metadata in mono.
  • Keep text in the 680 px reading column.

Don’t

  • Introduce a second brand color, gradients or drop shadows.
  • Use pure #ffffff or #000000.
  • Set text in tomato on light backgrounds; use --accent-text.
  • Use the light accent-text on dark bg, or vice versa.
  • Set text in --border, or weights above 600.
