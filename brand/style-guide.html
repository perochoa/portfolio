Design & style, master.

The single reference for how the Alexander Perocho brand looks, reads and behaves. It is taken directly from perocho.com, which is the canonical implementation. Use it for slides, résumés, social posts, documents, side-project sites and anything else that carries the name.

If this guide and the site ever disagree, the site wins: update this guide from src/styles/global.css.

01 Brand principles

Warm, not sterileOff-white paper and warm near-black instead of pure #fff/#000. Every neutral leans slightly warm.
One accentBurnt orange is the only brand color. It marks what matters: links, the active state, the signature word.
Quiet structureHairline borders and whitespace do the work. No shadows, gradients or heavy fills.
Engineer’s precisionMonospace for data and metadata, exact numbers, plain sentences. One italic serif word for a human touch.

Wordmark

  • Text “Alex Perocho” followed by a period in the accent color. The period is part of the mark; never drop it or recolor it to match the text.
  • Geist SemiBold (600), letter-spacing −0.02em, set on one line.
  • Use the full legal name “Alexander Perocho” in formal contexts (copyright lines, résumé header, page titles); the wordmark stays “Alex Perocho.”
  • On an accent background, the period switches to the text color (#1c1a17).

Monogram

  • Lowercase “ap” in Instrument Serif Italic, #fbfaf8 on #c2410c.
  • Rounded square, corner radius ≈ 22% of the side (14 / 64).
  • Use for favicons, app icons, social avatars where a photo doesn’t fit, and small sign-offs.
  • Minimum size 16 px. Keep clear space of at least 25% of the side on every edge.

03 Color

Six roles, each with a light and a dark value. Always design with the role (e.g. “muted”), not the hex, so both modes stay in step. Light mode is the default for print and documents; dark mode follows the viewer’s system setting on screens.

Supporting values

  • Accent soft --accent-soft: accent at 10% opacity (light, rgba(194,65,12,.10)) / 12% (dark, rgba(251,138,76,.12)). Background for badges and highlights only, with accent-colored text on top.
  • Status green #22c55e: only for the “available / live” pulsing dot. Not a brand color; never use it for text.
  • Selection: accent background with bg-colored text.
  • Header glass: bg at 85% opacity, backdrop-filter: saturate(1.5) blur(10px).

Rules

  • Weights in use: 400 body, 500 buttons/sub-roles/eyebrows, 600 headings and emphasis. Nothing heavier.
  • The serif appears once per view, italic, in accent, at 1.12× the surrounding size. Usually a name or a single key word.
  • Reading width (measure) is 680 px, about 70 characters.
  • Dates are written Mon D, YYYY (e.g. “Oct 19, 2026”) and set in mono.

Substitutes

Imagery

  • Images get the 10 px radius and a 1 px border; hero images sit in a surface-colored well, max 420 px tall, object-fit: contain.
  • Portraits are circular with a 1 px border, tilted −3°, rotating to +3° and 1.04× on hover.
  • Prefer real photos and real screenshots over stock or illustration. Warm, natural light suits the palette.
  • Icons: simple 16-unit glyphs at 18 px, currentColor, muted by default and accent on hover.

Motion

  • Default transition: 0.15 s ease on color, border, background and underline thickness.
  • Portrait: 0.3 s ease. Status dot pulse: 2.4 s ease-out, infinite.
  • Movement is small: 3 px arrow nudge, 3° tilt. Nothing slides in or bounces.
  • Respect prefers-reduced-motion: stop looping animations.
  • Focus: 2 px accent outline, 2 px offset, on every interactive element.

07 Voice & writing

  • First person, conversational, specific. “I build the infrastructure that gets machine learning models from training into production and keeps them healthy once they’re there.”
  • Lead with the answer. Post descriptions state the finding: “The answer was forever, for three separate reasons.”
  • Real numbers over adjectives. “6 EKS clusters across 2 AWS regions”, “$66K/yr”, not “many” or “significant”.
  • Name the tools. Kubernetes, Triton, Prometheus, Helm, Terraform, written with their official casing.
  • Sentence case for section headings, buttons and UI labels (“Recent writing”, “All posts →”). Title Case for blog post titles (“When Terraform Wants to Replace Every Node at Once”). Uppercase only for eyebrow labels.
  • Use colons and plain sentences for asides; avoid em-dash-heavy prose.
  • Arrows (→ ←) for directional links, middots (·) to separate metadata.
  • Tone: candid and a little self-deprecating about mistakes, never hype. No “passionate”, “rockstar”, “leveraging synergies”.

08 Applying the brand

Slides & presentations

  • Light bg #fbfaf8 by default; dark #121110 for evening talks or demos.
  • Title: Geist 600, −0.03em, with one serif accent word. Small mono eyebrow above (section name or date).
  • Big numbers styled like the stats row. Code in Geist Mono on surface with a hairline border.
  • Monogram bottom-left on content slides; wordmark on the title and closing slides.

Résumé & documents

  • Always light mode. Name in Geist 600, contact line in mono, muted.
  • Section headings as uppercase eyebrows with a trailing hairline (like “01 Brand principles” above).
  • Accent only for the name’s period, links and timeline markers. Body text stays #1c1a17.
  • Skills as chips or a mono comma list. Dates right-aligned in mono.

Social & thumbnails

  • OG / link images: 1200 × 630, bg or surface fill, post title in Geist 600, date in mono, wordmark bottom-left.
  • Accent-filled cards are allowed for announcements: #c2410c background, #fbfaf8 text, still one serif word.
  • Avatar: the portrait photo; fall back to the monogram.

Code, terminals & side projects

  • Syntax themes: GitHub Light / GitHub Dark (what the site uses via Shiki).
  • New sites: copy the tokens below verbatim, support prefers-color-scheme, no client JS unless it earns its place.
  • Set theme-color meta to #fbfaf8 / #121110.

Do

  • Use role tokens, and test both light and dark.
  • Keep accent under ~3% of the area.
  • Separate with whitespace and 1 px hairlines.
  • Put dates, versions and metadata in mono.
  • Keep text in the 680 px reading column.

Don’t

  • Introduce a second brand color, gradients or drop shadows.
  • Use pure #ffffff or #000000.
  • Use the serif for body text or more than once per view.
  • Use the light accent on dark bg, or vice versa.
  • Set text in --border, or weights above 600.
